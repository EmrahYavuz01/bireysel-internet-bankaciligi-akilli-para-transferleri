# Smart Transfers & Recurring Payments Management System

## Project Overview

This is an independent business analysis case study focused on individual internet banking. The project explores smart domestic payment routing, backup-asset-supported recurring payment instructions, and international SWIFT transfers.

The aim is to analyze banking business needs and translate them into structured requirements, business rules, user stories, acceptance criteria, process models, and interface prototypes.

## Business Scope

- **Smart Payment Routing:** Analysis of transfer routing based on payment type, recipient bank, transaction limits, and timing.
- **Recurring Payments with Backup Assets:** Analysis of recurring payment instructions that may use a predefined backup account or eligible liquid investment asset when the primary account has insufficient funds.
- **SWIFT Transfers:** Analysis of international transfer requirements, including beneficiary bank details, transfer charges, exchange-rate information, and payment tracking.

## Business Analysis Deliverables

- Business and functional requirements
- User stories and acceptance criteria
- Business rules
- Use cases
- BPMN and process flow diagrams
- Mockups and interface prototypes

## Project Purpose

This project demonstrates business analysis and requirements engineering skills within a retail banking context. It focuses on connecting business needs with structured requirements and process design.

## Project Status

This repository contains business analysis and design documentation prepared as an independent portfolio case study. It is not a live banking application, and production implementation or real banking integration is outside the current scope.
